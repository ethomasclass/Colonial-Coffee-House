# Colonial Coffee House — Guided Notes

*Scene-by-scene, in the order the patrons arrive. Two sheets, printed
double-sided. This is the **heavier** of the two student sheets — use it
**instead of** `STUDENT-HANDOUT.md`, not alongside it.*

**Which sheet to use**

| | `STUDENT-HANDOUT.md` | `GUIDED-NOTES.md` (this one) |
|---|---|---|
| Length | 1 sheet, 2 sides | 2 sheets, 4 sides |
| Shape | A table plus essay prompts | Fill-in-the-blank, one block per scene |
| Best for | A single 50-minute period, strong readers | Two periods, or a class that needs scaffolding |
| Written during play? | Partly | Almost entirely |

The blanks are deliberately one or two words. A student who is reading slowly
can still keep up, because the sentence around the blank carries the meaning
and the blank only asks for the term.
