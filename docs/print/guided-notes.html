Pull it together — 1

The Great Awakening and the Enlightenment were not the same movement. One was about religion; the other was about reason and government. But Cato says the preacher shouting in a field and the man in his book are asking the same question. Write that question in your own words, then explain what each of them is asking it about.

Use your own ledger as evidence. The Molasses Act had been law for eight years and had almost never been collected. Explain why — not "because colonists were lawless," but what the arithmetic on your counter actually did to an honest shopkeeper. Use the term salutary neglect.
