The Green Dragon

A Coffee House in Boston, 1741

You keep the coffee house on Union Street. Tonight six people will sit at your counter: a cooper who has just been converted, a minister who wishes he had not been, a printer's apprentice reading Locke by candlelight, a captain whose cargo will not survive an honest question, a merchant's daughter caught between them, and a customs officer who has been reading the books.

You have three shillings. Rent is five shillings and sixpence, due at closing. The cheapest sweetener on your shelf is the one the law says you may not buy.

Every drink here was really drunk in New England in the 1740s. None of them are alcoholic. Click any underlined term to see what it means.

Written for this game in the manner of the period press. The events reported — Whitefield on the Common, the dissolution of the Land Bank, the Molasses Act — are real.
