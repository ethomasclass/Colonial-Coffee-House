Sprite Conditioner

Image generators produce pixel-flavoured pictures: soft edges, thousands of near-identical colours, an inconsistent grid. This snaps one to a real pixel grid, cuts the palette down, and knocks out the background — so a generated portrait actually sits in the room instead of floating above it. Everything happens in this page; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Background

Samples the four corners. Works when the background is flat; if the generator gave you a room, ask it again for a plain one.
