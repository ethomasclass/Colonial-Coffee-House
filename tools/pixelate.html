Sprite Conditioner

Image generators make pixel-flavoured pictures: soft edges, thousands of near-identical colours, no consistent grid. This snaps one to a real pixel grid, cuts the palette to the game's own colours, and knocks out the background. It also slices a sheet of expressions into separate sprites that stay perfectly aligned with each other — which is the whole reason to draw them on one sheet. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Sheet of expressions

Cut into equal panels, read left to right, top to bottom, in this order: neutral, warm, worried, stern, blink — the five the game uses. Every panel is trimmed to the same box, so the head never jumps when the game swaps expressions mid-conversation.

Crop

Drag a box on the Before picture. Cut away a table the generator drew — the room has its own counter — and anything floating in a corner. On a sheet, crop to the panels themselves and the slicing follows.
